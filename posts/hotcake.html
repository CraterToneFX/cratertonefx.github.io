Crowther Hotcake

February 19, 2026 · Overdrive

There are 2 versions of this circuit on Guitar FX Layouts.
A buffered version and a non buffered version, the reason was because some people had issues with the circuit oscillating with presence and gain maxed.
Certain types of pickups could be a contributing factor. I chose to try the non buffered version and I didn't have any trouble with it. Not sure what type pickups were the issue but guessing active ones.




Otherwise good sounding overdrive.
