This is a guitar preamp circuit schematic that was created because I had a couple of the 6J1 tubes laying around.
They came from one of those preamp / headphone amp kits that you can find on AMAZON, EBAY, etc.
I wanted to do something with them for a while and finally got around to it.
So this is what I came up with.



It is 2 - 6j1 tubes wired as triodes with a Marshall style tonestack and gain control in between the stages.
It sounds pretty good for what it is. Slight breakup when it's by itself, very little.
When you put a distortion pedal in front of it it has some of that tube sound you would be looking for.
Definitely better sounding than I thought it was going to be.
It has plenty of output too, so far have only tested running it into an MXR headphone amp clone.
The volume has only been at about 2 for that, running it into an amp will be the real test I guess.
I'll eventually get to that.
Here is the BOM.



As far as powering this circuit, I used what I had...some salvaged but mostly new stuff
Back to back transformer setup. 2 small salvaged ones.
Pulled a line from 1st one for tube heater feed that goes to buck converter to get it down to 6.3vdc.
2nd one boosts the output up to 177vdc for the B+ feed for the tubes.
