Down the Rat Hole

August 29, 2026 · Distortion

This is another circuit that made it's way to my pedal board. Well at least one of them did.
I've tried a few different ProCo Rat circuits over time, one turned out really good and the others are ok.
This first one I tried a couple times, the first time had major squealing issue.
The second time I modified it to take other opamps and not squeal.
I made some notes to show what I changed and what was working.



The next circuit I tried was the best Rat circuit I put together, sounded way better than any of the others.
Although it did get the only good LM308 chip I had, so maybe that was part of it.
This one also had a bass control which was different. I can not find where I grabbed the layout from...



The last one I messed with is one that has three sets of diodes and a switch to switch between them.
It works well, the sets of diodes I tried had too much of a volume difference so I ended up removing the switch and just kept one set of diodes.
I used an UA741 opamp and the pinout on this diagram says LM741 but the pinout looks like it's from LM308.
You can see where I made notes on the image showing what I used.
This layout came from Guitar Related Ramblings
