CTFX Fuzz Face

Novenber 16, 2026 · CraterToneFX Circuits

This is my take on the silicon fuzz face circuit.
It is pretty much the same layout as most of them as you can see in the schematic.
Just different transistors, I used a C1675 and a C945. And some other changes and additions.
The hfe values mentioned are what those 2 are, it may be that any NPN silicon transistors with those numbers will work here.
Although I didn't try them to test it yet.



And here is the vero layout which can be considered verified.
I built it and is working great, reactive to guitar volume as it should be.
